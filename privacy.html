FrameMeter Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

FrameMeter is an Android app that shows a floating overlay with frame rate and device performance stats. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: FrameMeter itself does not collect, store on a server, or sell any of your data. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising service collects to show the banner ad on the settings screen.

Data FrameMeter handles on your phone

  • Performance stats (frame rate, frame times, CPU, GPU, memory, battery and temperature readings) and the name of the app currently on screen, which is used to measure its frame rate. These are read live, shown in the overlay, and never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Wireless debugging pairing. To measure real frame rates, FrameMeter connects to your phone's own Wireless debugging service. The pairing key it creates is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The connection is made to your own phone, not to any other device or server.
  • Your settings (which stats are shown, overlay position and size) are stored only on your phone. They are not included in device backups.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows one banner ad at the bottom of the app's settings screen. Ads are never shown in the overlay or over other apps. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process the following data to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK

Data is sent encrypted. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or in a region with similar laws, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in FrameMeter's settings list. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to draw the overlay.
  • Notifications: for the Show / Hide / Lock / Stop controls.
  • Foreground service: keeps the overlay running while you use other apps.
  • Internet and network state: to connect to your phone's own Wireless debugging service, and to load ads.
  • Change secure settings (only if you turn on Auto-reconnect): lets FrameMeter switch Wireless debugging on and off for you. It changes no other settings.

Children

FrameMeter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
